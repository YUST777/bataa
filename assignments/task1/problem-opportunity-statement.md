# Bataa: Problem and Opportunity Statement

Beginners who try to learn Blender, Godot, Unity, Premiere or After Effects by watching video tutorials often cannot finish a project. They feel stuck because they must replicate every step on the screen while the video is still running.

Now they pause the video, rewind, and look on forums for the missing click. This takes 20 to 30 hours on their first Blender project, and only 5 to 15 percent of people finish a free video course.

We learned this by talking to 5 to 10 learners on campus. They said they cannot follow the instructor, they cannot find the part he points at, and it is too hard to work out by themselves.

Now is the right time because AI can see the learner's screen and show the click. People already pay for video courses like Yanfaa, which starts at EGP 25, so they would pay for a subscription to finish.

## Exercise 1: strip the solution

**Our idea in one sentence:** Someone sits next to you while you learn a program, shows you each click, and checks that you get it right.

**In plain words:** A patient teacher on your own screen any time of day.

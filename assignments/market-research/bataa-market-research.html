06 · PESTEL

What's happening around us

Political

  • Egypt pushes digital skills through ITIDA, Creativa and Digital Egypt
  • Government support for local startups

Economic

  • Weak pound makes learners careful with money
  • AI costs are paid in dollars
  • Freelancing pays in dollars, so skills are worth it

Social

  • 3.9M university students, young and online
  • Learning from YouTube is normal
  • Many quit courses halfway

Technological

  • AI can now see a screen and point at buttons

Environmental

  • Learning from home, no travel to centers
  • AI servers use a lot of energy

Legal

  • Egypt's data protection law (Law 151 of 2020): screen data must be handled carefully
  • We use software names, not their logos

07 · SWOT

Where we stand

Strengths

  • Our edge: guidance in the real app
  • The desktop agent is fully built. The mobile app, website and APK are live
  • Already made revenue. One customer's video got 300K views on his Instagram, made by Bataa
  • Students across faculties were excited

Weaknesses

  • Only Blender projects for now
  • Little data from real users so far

Opportunities

  • Self-learners worldwide, starting with 3.9M students in Egypt
  • More apps: Godot, Unity, Premiere, After Effects, VS Code
  • Platform commission: share of sales on creator and institution projects
  • Later: software teams (API)

Threats

  • Users worry about sharing their screen

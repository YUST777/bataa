# Reverse-Engineered Onboarding Patterns (Mimo & Duolingo 2022 vs 2024)

## 1. Funnel Architecture

### Screen 1: Zero-Friction Hook
- Character/Mascot greets user with clear value proposition.
- 1 primary CTA: 'Get Started' (or 'ابدأ الآن مجاناً').
- 1 text link: 'I already have an account' (Log in).

### Screen 2: Track & Tool Selection
- Cards with clear iconography and beginner tags.
- In Bataa: 'Blender 3D Artist', 'Python & AI Developer', 'Full-Stack Developer'.

### Screen 3: Motive & Identity Alignment
- Question: 'Why are you learning?'
- Options:
  1. Switch careers / Land a tech job
  2. Level up in my current role
  3. Build my own apps/games (Founder/Hobbyist)
  4. Learn for fun & school

### Screen 4: 60-Second Interactive Hands-on Preview
- Instant interactive micro-challenge.
- In Bataa: Place a glowing yellow box over 'Create Cube' in simulated Blender window.
- When clicked, user receives instant success chime and XP.

### Screen 5: Streak Commitment Contract
- 'Pick your daily goal':
  - Casual: 5 min/day
  - Regular: 15 min/day
  - Serious: 30 min/day
- Pledge: 'Commit to a 3-Day Streak'.

### Screen 6: 7-Day Trial Soft Paywall (No Surprise Billing)
- Visual 3-step timeline:
  - Today: Start 7-day free trial.
  - Day 5: Email/notification reminder before trial ends.
  - Day 7: Billing begins (Cancel anytime).

### Screen 7: Active Lesson & Delayed Account Creation
- Seamlessly transition to Lesson 1.
- Prompts Google 1-tap or Email signup only when saving progress after Lesson 1.
